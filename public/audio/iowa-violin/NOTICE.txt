University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios — Musical Instrument Samples
Solo violin, pianissimo arco, performed by Leonid Iogansen, recorded December 11, 2011.
Collection created by Lawrence Fritts. The collection page permits use for any
project without restrictions. These recordings are not designated CC0 here.
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISviolin2012.html

Fifteen exact-pitch recordings were split from the mono AIFF chromatic scales
Violin.arco.pp.sulG.C4B4, sulA.A4B5, sulE.E5B5, and sulE.C6B6, then saved as
mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV without pitch or gain processing. Filenames
indicate MIDI pitch. The natural recordings play without sustain loops.
