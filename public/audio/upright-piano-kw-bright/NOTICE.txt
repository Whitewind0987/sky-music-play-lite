Nine FLAC files were losslessly converted to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV.
Sharp signs in filenames were replaced with "s". PCM samples were unchanged.
The player applies the original SFZ pitch ranges and continuous loop points.
It uses the SFZ's 0.6 s release for explicit note durations and retains a
shorter release for implicit notes.
