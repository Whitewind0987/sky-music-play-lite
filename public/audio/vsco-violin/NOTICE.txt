Nine stereo 44.1 kHz 16-bit PCM files retain their original bowed attack.
The source SFZ and WAVs do not define sustain loops. For each sample, the
120 ms before frame 44100 was matched against stereo waveform windows ending
between seconds 2 and 3 using waveform correlation and energy similarity.
The chosen ending was crossfaded into that window
with a raised-cosine curve and the unused recording tail was removed.
Playback loops from frame 44100 to the end of the resulting file.
Playback applies the SFZ's -20 cent tuning correction to the C4 sample region.
